# Community culture workflow

Actual workflow clarified by Magnus on 23 October 2026. This replaces the old
planning SOP as the description of what was done. Use each run's saved well map,
OD inputs and Echo report for its strains, doses and transfer status.

## Procedure

1. Grow precultures in nutrient-rich medium for **24 hours**.
2. Measure OD600 and dilute the precultures to prepare the OD-based source
   suspensions used by that run's picklist. **Do not wash the cells.**
3. Transfer the calculated inoculum into **crude-glycerol screen medium** using
   the Echo picklist.
4. Incubate for **48 hours**.
5. Spin the cultures down with the centrifuge set to **0 °C**.
6. Use the supernatant in the [lactate assay](SOP%20%E2%80%94%20Colorimetric%20L-Lactate%20Assay%20for%20Bacterial%20Supernatants.md).

No pre-inoculation pelleting, two washes, resuspension of washed pellets,
sonication, filtration or additional supernatant cleanup is part of this
reported workflow. Dilution is not washing: nutrient-medium components and
preculture metabolites can accompany the inoculum. Their amounts were not
measured by this clarification.

## Current dosing convention

For the October 21 diagnostic run, the saved design uses:

- **50 µL crude-glycerol medium per destination well, plus inoculum**.
- **0.100 OD600·µL per member**, rounded to a **25 nL** Echo droplet multiple.
- Working source OD600 target **1.20**; lower-density precultures remain undiluted.
- Source dilution recipe uses **screen medium without glycerol**.
- **50 µL total per source well**, with demand checked against source dead volume.

These are recorded design settings, not proof of physical delivery. The
community receives more total starting OD-equivalent biomass than a monoculture.
Do not describe this as equal total biomass across community sizes. Earlier
rounds must be reconstructed from their own saved inputs and full transfer logs.

## Medium recipes on record

The historical nutrient-medium recipe is **10 g/L yeast extract + 10 g/L
soytone**, adjusted to pH 7.0. The screen-medium recipe on record is modified
M9-K with **100 mM total phosphate** and a target **3 g/L crude-glycerol
equivalent**. The full salts, stock preparation and batch assumptions remain in
the [archived recipe](archive/20261023_before_operator_correction/community_assembly_planned.md).

These recipes are not batch certificates. For matrix troubleshooting, verify
the actual batch composition, crude-glycerol lot/content and preparation against
laboratory records. Do not treat the old theoretical glycerol conversion or
buffer-capacity calculation as a measured lactate concentration or final pH.

## What is not established by this workflow

Incubator temperature, shaking/orbit, plate seal, actual oxygen availability,
centrifugal force and duration are not supplied in the October 23 clarification.
The older assay notes say "4 min max speed" and cooling set to 0 °C ten minutes
before use; retain that as a prior note, not a calibrated RCF measurement.

Endpoint OD, pH measurements, plate weighing, t=0 lactate measurements,
glycerol-consumption assays and D-lactate assays must not be described as having
been performed merely because an old plan requested them. Populated raw records
are needed. Forty-eight hours is the chosen endpoint, not proof of stationary
phase or of a universal stationary-phase lactate-production mechanism.
