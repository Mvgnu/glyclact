# Experimental protocols and records

The two SOP files in this directory describe the actual routine clarified by
Magnus on **23 October 2026**. Start there, not with an old experiment plan.

- `SOP — Community Assembly and Crude-Glycerol Fermentation Screen.md`: 24 h
  nutrient-rich preculture, OD-based dilution without washing, crude-glycerol
  culture for 48 h, cold spin.
- `SOP — Colorimetric L-Lactate Assay for Bacterial Supernatants.md`: 5 µL
  supernatant + 10 µL crude-glycerol medium, 15 µL reagent mix, one hour protected
  from light, 15 µL 1 M acetic acid, absorbance read.
- `archive/20261023_before_operator_correction/`: unchanged older planning SOPs
  and the October 2 pipetting guide. Retained reagent/media recipes; unperformed
  steps in these files are not experimental facts.
- `echo/readout/`: available original reader exports, including October 2/3.
- `echo/transfer_logs/`: original Echo full reports and exception lists.
- `echo/experiment_setup_output/`: frozen designs, source ODs, picklists and
  recording templates. Empty fields are missing data, not zeros.
- `echo/readout_analysis/`: previous analyses/figures, to be independently checked.
