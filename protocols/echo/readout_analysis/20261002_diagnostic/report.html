Culture replicates

The high community signals concentrate in R5 and R6. This could reflect culture variation or handling/position effects. This run cannot assign the cause. Some high pairs disagree greatly even though they should contain the same liquid.

Analytical checks

Spike preparation actually reported

7.5 uL of 12 mM lactate + 5 uL medium + 2.5 uL undiluted supernatant = 15 uL. Added lactate is 6 mM before reagents, or 2 mM in a 45 uL stopped well. The sample fraction is 1/6, compared with 1/3 if ordinary wells followed 5 + 10 uL. Consequently, spike-minus-unspiked is not conventional matched recovery.

If the lactate stock was in water, half the spike aliquot is water and the medium matrix is also changed. If it was in fresh medium, only the spent/fresh medium ratio changes. Without a useful calibration, no percentage recovery is reported. The brief operator reply did not distinguish the two possible spike-stock matrices.

What this supports

  • Two community compositions contain the largest measured signals. "Only monocultures produced" is not supported by the saved map.
  • 41 and 75 have relatively modest, consistent raw signals; the two leading communities are more variable between cultures.
  • The signals are A490 absorbance, not demonstrated lactate concentrations. Flat mapped standards and duplicate disagreement prevent a clean production or synergy claim.
  • Retain raw files, culture identity, individual aliquots and both reads. Do not use these values as calibrated lactate training labels yet.
  • Endpoint growth cannot be evaluated because no populated OD readout was supplied.
