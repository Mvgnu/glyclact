# Model gate results — pool v2 (brief gate 2)

Generated by `audit_models.py`; full table in `results/model_gate.tsv`. Mineral base
is strictly carbon-free; glycerol uptake fixed at 10 mmol/gDW/h, so yields read
directly as mol product / mol glycerol. This supersedes the gate on the retired
six-member panel.

## Verdict

**Nine of ten models pass. One fails on a specific, fixable transport gap.**

Unlike the old panel, the producers can secrete lactate: all five reach the
carbon-limited ceiling of 1.00 mol L-lactate per mol glycerol.

## What passed, for all ten

- **No free lunch.** With every carbon exchange closed, no model produces biomass
  or any product.
- **No growth without carbon**, aerobic or anaerobic.
- **Mass balance sound.** 1–5 element-level imbalances per model, all H±1 on
  siderophore/PTS transporters inherited from the BiGG universe, plus the biomass
  pseudo-reaction. Charge imbalances (232–532) are a known universe artefact and do
  not affect FBA.
- **Thermodynamically correct ceilings.** Max L-lactate is exactly 1.00 mol/mol
  glycerol wherever export exists.

## The one failure

**`P3_G9` cannot use glycerol.** It grows well on glucose (1.347) and on
glycerol-3-phosphate (0.703), and it has `GLYK` to phosphorylate glycerol in the
cytosol — but **no inner-membrane glycerol transport at all**. `GLYCtex` carries
glycerol into the periplasm and nothing moves it to the cytosol, so `GLYK` has no
substrate. `GLYCtpp` and `GLYCt` are both absent.

This matters because `P3_G9` is one of only two prototrophic, catalase-positive,
non-lactate-consuming supporters in the library. On glycerol as sole carbon source
it cannot establish, so it could only ever grow on a partner's excretions.

The gap is a strong candidate for curation rather than rejection: glycerol is
famously membrane-permeable by passive diffusion, and the model already has both
the outer-membrane step and the kinase. Adding a facilitated-diffusion `GLYCtpp`
restores a route that biophysics supplies anyway.

## Lactate export runs through a malate antiporter, not a lactate exporter

Worth recording because it is not obvious from the reaction names. **Every
`L_LACt2r` in the pool is import-only** — `h_e + lac__L_e --> h_c + lac__L_c` with
bounds [0, 1000]. Lactate leaves by a different route:

- `P3_F11`, `P1_B11`: the only reaction producing `lac__L_e` is `MALt10`, a
  malate/lactate antiporter. In the optimal solution `MALt10` runs at +10 while
  `MALt3r` runs at −10, exporting malate back out — an internal malate cycle driven
  by the proton gradient, with `LDH_L` at −10 producing lactate. Blocking malate
  exchange entirely does **not** reduce the yield, because the cycle is internal.
- `P1_B3` additionally has `LACLt2`, a directly reversible `lac__L_e <=> lac__L_c`,
  so it does not depend on the antiport route.
- `P3_C11`, `P3_C9`, `P3_G9` have `L_LACt3` (`h_e + lac__L_c --> h_c + lac__L_e`),
  a genuine export reaction.

The mechanism is stoichiometrically valid, self-contained, and a real biochemical
motif (malolactic-type antiport), so it should behave the same under COMETS. But it
means lactate efflux is coupled to ion gradients that dFBA does not represent
faithfully, and measured efflux may not match.

## Behaviours that are correct by design, not failures

- **`P3_A10` has no lactate exchange at all** — which is exactly what the screen
  predicted from its missing lactate-transporter annotation. The gate independently
  confirms the `unannotated_gap` call, so that heuristic is doing real work.
- **`P2_D12`'s lactate transport is import-only** (`L_LACt2rpp` forward =
  periplasm → cytosol; `D_LACt2rpp` absent), giving max lactate 0.00. It matches its
  assigned role as the rich supporter that consumes lactate, and independently
  corroborates that `lctP`/`lldP`-family permeases are catabolic carriers.
- **`P1_G5`** has no lactate exchange — a pure sink, as intended.

## Two findings that constrain the community design

1. **No model can both export DHA and make lactate, so the shuttle does not close.**
   Only `P3_C9` and `P2_D12` carry `EX_dha_e` at all, and testing secretion rather
   than mere presence shows:

   | Genome | Max DHA secreted | Max G3P secreted | Grows on DHA | Makes lactate |
   |---|---:|---:|:---:|:---:|
   | `P3_C9` | **0.00** | 0.00 | yes (0.100) | yes (1.00) |
   | `P2_D12` | **1.00** | 0.87 | yes (0.565) | no (0.00) |
   | all others | no exchange | 0.00 | – | varies |

   `P3_C9` has the exchange but **cannot export** DHA — it only imports. The pool's
   only DHA exporter is `P2_D12`, which cannot make lactate and consumes it. So the
   shuttle needs curation on **both** sides, and the donor side is the harder one.
2. **The non-producers grow 5–7× faster on glycerol than the producers.** `P1_G5`
   at 0.703 and `P2_D12` at 0.640 against 0.100–0.129 for the producers. In a
   shared-glycerol COMETS run the sink and the rich supporter will dominate
   substrate uptake and biomass. This is a first-order effect on the presence/absence
   landscape and should be expected in the results rather than discovered in them.

## What curating the DHA shuttle actually requires

CarveMe is not missing the *ability* to represent DHA transport. The bacteria
universe (5531 reactions) already contains `DHAt`, `DHAtpp`, `DHAtex`, `EX_dha_e`,
`DHAK` and `DHAPT`. They were not selected because DHA transport has no dedicated
gene — it rides on the `glpF` aquaglyceroporin, which CarveMe maps to `GLYCtpp`
only. So the two sides of the shuttle are not equally hard:

- **Receiver side: re-enabling existing universe reactions, with gene evidence.**
  Add `DHAtpp`/`DHAt` + `DHAtex` + `EX_dha_e` to a strain that has both `glpF`
  (aquaglyceroporins are documented to pass DHA) and an annotated DHA kinase.
  `P1_B3` satisfies both — `glpF` present and a confident `dhaK` call — and the
  kinase reaction `DHAK` is already in its model. This is well-founded curation, not
  invention.
- **Donor side: one reaction has to be authored.** The membrane-bound
  PQQ-dependent glycerol dehydrogenase that puts DHA in the periplasm has no BiGG
  template, because the universe contains essentially no Acetobacteraceae
  periplasmic-oxidation biochemistry. `P3_C9`/`P3_C11` will not export DHA until a
  periplasmic `glyc_p + ubiquinone -> dha_p` reaction is added by hand, paired with
  the universe's `DHAtex`.

`glpF` evidence across the pool, which is what licenses the receiver-side addition:

| Has `glpF` | No `glpF` |
|---|---|
| `P1_B3`, `P1_B11`, `P3_F11`, `P2_D12`, `P3_B12` (×2) | `P3_A10`, `P3_C11`, `P3_C9`, `P1_G5` (`aqpZ` only) |

CarveMe placed `GLYCtpp` in precisely the `glpF`-positive strains, which is why the
`P3_G9` gap was faithful rather than a bug.
